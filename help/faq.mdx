## What Tradion does

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Tradion place trades?" icon="ban">
    No. Never, on any plan, under any setting. There is no order-entry path in the product: nothing in Tradion can buy, sell, size, or close a position for you. Every output is analysis that you act on yourself, through your own broker.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is this investment advice?" icon="scale-balanced">
    No. Tradion Labs is not a registered investment adviser, a broker-dealer, or a financial institution, and nothing in the product is a personalised recommendation to buy or sell anything. Tradion produces analysis (a rating, a level, a pattern in your own history) and the decision is entirely yours. Trading involves risk of loss.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to connect a brokerage?" icon="link-slash">
    No. A connection saves typing: your positions and closed trades arrive on their own. Without one you enter positions and trades by hand, and every other part of the product behaves the same way. See [portfolio overview](/portfolio/overview).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the difference between Lens and Chart Analysis?" icon="crosshairs">
    Lens reads a live chart inside Tradion. You open a chart in the app, start a capture, and Lens combines what it sees with current prices, indicators, and upcoming events, then takes follow-up questions. It needs Chrome or Edge on a desktop, and it reads Tradion's own chart only: it cannot capture a TradingView tab or anything else on your screen.

    [Chart Analysis](/research/chart-analyzer) takes a chart image you already have and reads the image, nothing else. It returns BUY, SELL, HOLD, or NO TRADE in a mode you pick (Swing, Day Trade, or Scalp), with no follow-up questions. Both are on every plan with no monthly cap. Full comparison: [Lens](/research/lens).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Signing in

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is there two-factor authentication?" icon="shield-halved">
    Not on the Tradion account itself, because there is no password to protect. You sign in with Google or with a magic link sent to your email, so whatever protects that email account is what protects your Tradion account. Turn on 2FA there.

    Two-factor prompts you may see inside Tradion belong to your **broker**, not to us, and appear while connecting or reconnecting a brokerage.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I sign out of a device I no longer have?" icon="right-from-bracket">
    Sign out from that device if you still can. There is no device list and no revoke-everywhere button.

    A session lasts **7 days** and then expires on its own. A magic link is single use and short-lived, so an old email cannot be reused to get back in. If you think someone has access to the email address on the account, secure that mailbox first, since it is the way back into Tradion.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Your brokerage connection

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What can Tradion see, and can it move my money?" icon="lock">
    The link runs through SnapTrade, a regulated aggregator (a service that holds the connection to your broker on your behalf). The access granted is read-only: balances, positions, and transaction history come in, and nothing goes out. Tradion cannot place, change, or cancel an order, and cannot move money. It does not receive your personal details, your bank links, or your tax documents, and it never sees your broker password. Revoke the connection at **Settings → Connected Brokerages**, and again inside your broker's own security settings. See [connecting a brokerage](/portfolio/connect-brokerage).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which brokers are supported?" icon="building-columns">
    More than 30. Commonly used ones include Interactive Brokers, Charles Schwab, Fidelity, E*TRADE, Robinhood, Webull, Tradier, Alpaca, and Coinbase. Search the full list inside the connection window. If yours is not there, log trades by hand and [tell us which one](/help/contact).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Plans, limits, and billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is there a free plan?" icon="tag">
    No. Three plans, all paid: Starter $25, Trader $100, Quant $200 a month. Starter and Trader include a 7-day free trial, once per account. An account with no subscription cannot run analyses. See [plan comparison](/reference/plan-comparison).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when I hit a limit?" icon="gauge-high">
    You get a clear block naming the meter you ran out of, not a silent failure. Two things are metered: AI Portfolio Analyst messages and AI Earnings Research messages.

    Out of AI Portfolio Analyst messages, your existing sessions still open. Out of earnings messages, your earlier research threads and notebook still open. See [usage limits](/concepts/usage-limits).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do unused credits roll over?" icon="rotate-left">
    No. Both meters reset to the full monthly allowance on your billing cycle date, which is the day of the month you subscribed rather than the 1st. Whatever you did not use is gone. A month where you used 4 of 50 earnings messages resets to 50, not 96.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I get a refund?" icon="receipt">
    Subscription fees are non-refundable, including for the unused part of a period, and there is no credit for a mid-cycle downgrade or for unused allowances. If you think you have been billed in error, email contact@tradionlabs.com within 30 days of the charge and we will look at it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Your data

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do you train AI models on my trading data?" icon="database">
    Yes, in one specific way.

    Every time you save a **Trade Autopsy**, which is Tradion's written breakdown of one closed trade, two labelled training examples are derived from it and stored automatically. One pairs the trade's context with the behavioural mistake the autopsy found. The other pairs the same context with the quality scores it gave. This happens on save, there is no separate opt-in, and it cannot be switched off while your account is active.

    If those examples are used to fine-tune a model, your user ID and other identifying information are stripped first. Anonymised data that has already gone into a training batch cannot be pulled back out afterwards.

    The examples are not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with third parties for their own purposes. The AI providers that generate your analyses (Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI) state in their own API terms that they do not train on data sent through their APIs.

    Email contact@tradionlabs.com to have your training examples exported, deleted, or flagged so they are left out of any future fine-tuning. Full detail is in the [Privacy Policy](https://tradionlabs.com/privacy).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I turn personalisation off?" icon="toggle-off">
    Yes. A **Personalization On / Off** switch sits in the status bar at the top of the app. The same switch also lives on your [Profile](/trade-intelligence/profile). It is the same setting in both places, so toggling one toggles the other. Turned off, none of your history is written into AI requests and answers come back generic. It applies everywhere, immediately.

    It stops your profile being sent into prompts. It does not stop Tradion building and updating that profile. The data is collected either way.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you sell my trading data?" icon="shield-halved">
    No. Tradion does not sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal information, and that includes your trades, your positions, and your behavioural profile. Your data goes only to the service providers needed to run the product, such as the payment processor and the AI providers that generate your analyses. The full list is in the [Privacy Policy](https://tradionlabs.com/privacy).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I export my data?" icon="download">
    There is no one-click account export in the app yet. Email contact@tradionlabs.com and ask for a copy of your data in a machine-readable format. Do this *before* deleting an account, not after.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my data if I cancel?" icon="box-archive">
    It stays. Cancelling ends the billing and drops you to an unsubscribed account at the end of the period you have paid for. Your trades, autopsies, analyses, memory, and playbook rules are all still there when you come back.

    Deleting your account is the separate, permanent action. [That page explains the difference](/account/deleting-your-account).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Using the product

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is there a watchlist?" icon="list">
    No. Tradion has no list of symbols you keep and check. Your [Portfolio](/portfolio/overview) shows what you hold. For any other ticker, ask the [AI Portfolio Analyst](/portfolio/ai-portfolio-analyst), or open it in [Chart Analysis](/research/chart-analyzer) or [Lens](/research/lens).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Tradion on my phone?" icon="mobile">
    Not for the main product. Signing in works on a phone, but any browser window narrower than 640 pixels gets a screen asking you to move to a desktop, and that screen offers to email you a sign-in link for your computer. It is a width check rather than a device check, so a tablet in landscape usually passes and the same tablet in portrait may not.

    Charts, the AI Portfolio Analyst, and the research workspaces are wide multi-panel screens. A phone can display them without giving you room to work in them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How current is the market data?" icon="clock">
    Live quotes and snapshots refresh on a 60-second cache, and the historical bars behind indicators on a 5-minute one. Slower-moving data is held longer: company overviews and economic series for an hour, earnings history for four. See [data sources](/concepts/data-sources).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How many trades before the behavioural analysis is useful?" icon="chart-line">
    About ten reviewed trades. Patterns are found by comparing autopsies against each other, so below roughly ten the reports are about individual trades, and above it they start being about you. A short psychological profile unlocks at two completed autopsies, and trend arrows and score changes appear at twenty.

    Logging a trade and reviewing it are not the same thing. A hundred logged trades with three autopsies still reads as thin. See [Trade Autopsy](/trade-intelligence/trade-autopsy).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What AI models do you use?" icon="microchip">
    Anthropic's Claude does the bulk of the work: Sonnet for chart analysis, the AI Portfolio Analyst, trade autopsies, and AI Earnings Research, Haiku for short follow-ups and summaries, and Opus for scoring news sentiment. Google's Gemini handles some lightweight extraction jobs. OpenAI is used only to turn text into the numeric form that powers search, never for chat or analysis. Every plan uses the same models; the difference between plans is the monthly allowance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there an API?" icon="plug">
    No. There is no public API, there are no API keys, and support cannot issue you one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
