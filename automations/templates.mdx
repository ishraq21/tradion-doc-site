<Info>
  Automations need the **Trader** plan or above. See [plans](/reference/plan-comparison).
</Info>

Click **Create Automation → Choose a Template** and you'll see these 45 options grouped into seven categories. Each one drops a fully wired canvas — signals, agents, channels, cooldown, and all. Change the ticker first; everything else is a starting point, not a lock.

The difficulty label reflects how many moving parts are involved, not how hard the concept is.

## Price Action

Conditions that watch raw price movement: breakouts, gaps, and crashes.

| Template | What fires it | Agent | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Gap Detector** | Daily move up 5%+ | Chart Analyst | Beginner |
| **Breakout Alert** | Price crosses above a level you set | Fundamental Analyst | Beginner |
| **Crash Alert** | Daily move down 8%+ | Research Agent | Beginner |
| **Support Bounce** | Price crosses below a level while RSI is under 35 | Chart Analyst | Intermediate |
| **Volume Breakout** | Daily move up 3%+ with 5× average volume | Fundamental Analyst | Intermediate |

## Technical

Indicator-driven setups: crossovers, extremes, and volatility compression.

| Template | What fires it | Agent | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **RSI Oversold Entry** | RSI crosses below 30 | Chart Analyst | Beginner |
| **RSI Overbought Warning** | RSI crosses above 70 | Sentiment Analyst | Beginner |
| **MACD Bullish Crossover** | MACD crosses above zero | Chart Analyst | Beginner |
| **Bollinger Squeeze** | Bollinger Bandwidth drops below 4 | Chart Analyst | Intermediate |
| **Golden Cross** | 50-day SMA crosses above 200-day | Fundamental Analyst | Intermediate |
| **Death Cross Warning** | 50-day SMA crosses below 200-day | Research Agent | Intermediate |
| **Stochastic Reversal** | Stochastic below 20 AND RSI below 40 | Chart Analyst | Intermediate |

## Event-Driven

Corporate events: earnings proximity, news, insider trades, and corporate actions.

| Template | What fires it | Agent | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Earnings Approaching** | Earnings report within 7 days | Research Agent | Beginner |
| **News Catalyst** | News detected AND price up 2%+ | Sentiment Analyst | Intermediate |
| **Insider Buying Detected** | SEC Form 4 purchase filed | Research Agent | Intermediate |
| **Insider Selling Warning** | Insider sale filed AND price down 3%+ | Research Agent + Report Writer | Advanced |
| **Corporate Action Alert** | Dividend, split, or merger detected | Fundamental Analyst | Beginner |

Momentum, crash, and volatility setups on crypto pairs.

| Template | What fires it | Agent | Difficulty |

| **Crypto Momentum** | RSI above 50 AND MACD above zero | Chart Analyst | Beginner |
| **Crypto Crash Alert** | Daily move down 15%+ | Sentiment Analyst | Beginner |
| **Altcoin Pump Detector** | Daily move up 20%+ | Sentiment Analyst | Beginner |
| **Crypto Bollinger Squeeze** | Bollinger Bandwidth drops below 3 | Chart Analyst | Intermediate |

Portfolio protection: drawdown monitoring, volatility spikes, and scheduled reviews.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| **Volatility Spike Warning** | ATR crosses above 5 AND price down 3%+ | Fundamental Analyst | Intermediate |

## Multi-Agent Strategies

Three-agent pipelines that research, analyse, and synthesise before the message goes out. Each run uses multiple agent credits — see [usage limits](/concepts/usage-limits).

| Template | What fires it | Agents | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Full Due Diligence** | Price up 4%+ | Research + Fundamental + Report Writer | Advanced |
| **Earnings Deep Dive** | Up 5%+ within 7 days of earnings | Research + Sentiment + Report Writer | Advanced |
| **Momentum Trifecta** | RSI above 50 AND MACD above zero AND ADX above 25 | Research + Fundamental + Report Writer | Advanced |
| **Mean Reversion Screen** | RSI below 25 AND MFI below 20 AND price below lower Bollinger band | Research + Sentiment + Report Writer | Advanced |
| **Smart Money Tracker** | Insider buying AND CMF above 0.05 AND 3× volume | Research + Fundamental + Report Writer | Advanced |
| **Breakout Confirmation** | Up 3%+ AND RSI above 55 AND ADX above 20 AND 3× volume | Research + Chart Analyst + Report Writer | Advanced |
| **Risk-Off Protocol** | Down 5%+ AND RSI below 30 AND ATR spike | Research + Sentiment + Report Writer | Advanced |
| **News Momentum Cascade** | News detected AND up 3%+ AND 3× volume | Sentiment + Research + Report Writer | Advanced |

## Scheduled Briefs

Time-based automations that fire on a clock. Most use a Market asset with no signals — the schedule alone triggers them.

| Template | When it fires | Agent | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Morning Market Brief** | Weekdays 9:25 AM ET | Market Brief | Beginner |
| **Pre-Market Scanner** | Weekdays 8:00 AM ET | Market Brief | Beginner |
| **Power Hour Brief** | Weekdays 3:00 PM ET | Market Brief | Beginner |
| **Post-Close Wrap** | Weekdays 4:15 PM ET | Chart Analyst | Beginner |
| **Weekly Portfolio Review** | Fridays 4:30 PM ET | Market Brief | Beginner |
| **Weekend Watchlist Scan** | Sundays 7:00 PM ET | Research Agent | Intermediate |
| **Crypto Morning Brief** | Daily 8:00 AM ET | Market Brief | Beginner |
| **Crypto Weekend Brief** | Saturdays 10:00 AM ET | Market Brief | Beginner |
| **Earnings Week Prep** | Sundays 6:00 PM ET | Research Agent | Intermediate |
| **Monthly Deep Dive** | First Monday of the month 9:00 AM ET | Research + Fundamental + Report Writer | Advanced |
| **Morning Gap Check** | Weekdays 9:35 AM ET — only if price up 2%+  | Chart Analyst | Intermediate |
| **Daily RSI Check** | Weekdays 10:00 AM ET — only if RSI below 30 | Fundamental Analyst | Intermediate |

<Note>
  Morning Gap Check and Daily RSI Check are scheduled automations with a signal condition. The schedule gates when the check happens, and the signal decides whether it fires. See [the Schedule node](/automations/overview#the-schedule-node).
</Note>
