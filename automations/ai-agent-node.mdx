A price alert tells you a number moved. An Agent node tells you what was going on around it: drop one between your signals and your Action node and Tradion researches the ticker before the message goes out.

## The six you can add

Click **+ Agent** in the toolbar. Each type can be added once, so six is the ceiling. They run one after another, and the Report Writer is always moved last.

| Agent | Reads | Writes | Reach for it when | Variable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Research Agent** | Filings, earnings transcripts, news, the web | Findings plus catalysts (events that could move the price) | You want the story behind an event | `{research_agent}` |
| **Fundamental Analyst** | Statements, analyst estimates, price history | Valuation, growth, margins, earnings quality, peers | Earnings are close | `{fundamental_agent}` |
| **Sentiment Analyst** | News tone, Reddit, insider filings, options chain | A positive/negative score, split by source | A name moves on chatter, not numbers | `{sentiment_agent}` |
| **Chart Analyst** | A chart image rendered on the spot | A BUY / SELL / WAIT read with levels | The setup is technical | `{chart_agent}` |
| **Market Brief** | Indices, macro, sectors, your holdings | A whole-market digest, not one ticker | You want a briefing on a schedule | `{market_brief_agent}` |
| **Report Writer** | The agents above | One memo assembled from all of it | Two or more others are on the canvas | `{report_agent}` |

The five agents above are **worker agents**: each does its own research and produces its own findings. The **Report Writer** is the **orchestrator** — it does no research of its own, waits for the workers to finish, and writes the memo from what they returned. That is why it always sits last on the canvas.

## How long it takes

Agents are sequential, so the wait is their sum. Market Brief takes about 6 minutes, cut off at 12. Research about 3, Fundamental about 2, and Chart, Sentiment and Report Writer 1 to 2 each, all cut off at 5. The Action node prints the total: **Est. ~15 min · 6 agents, sequential**.

### In plain English

An agent buys context and costs speed. The alert waits for the research, so it lands minutes after the trigger rather than seconds. Too slow for a scalp. Fine for a swing setup or an earnings run-up.

## Settings, agent by agent

Every agent also has an **Instructions** box, free text capped at 500 characters.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Research Agent">
    **Sources** (five, first three on by default): filings, earnings transcripts, news and sentiment, corporate actions (dividends, splits, mergers), web search.

    **Lookback**, how far back it searches: 1Q, 2Q, 1Y (default), or 2Y.

    **Focus** (optional): Revenue Growth, Debt Analysis, Management Changes, Risk Factors, Competitive Position, Margin Trends. Leave empty for a general read, or pick two or three to pull the brief that way.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Fundamental Analyst">
    **Depth**: Quick (key ratios), Standard (adds earnings and balance sheet), or Deep Dive (multi-quarter trends plus the wider economy). Standard is the default.

    **Style**, which decides what the write-up leads with: Full Synthesis (default), Value Focus, Growth Focus, or Quality Focus.

    **Peers** (optional): comma-separated tickers to compare against. Fill this in if you want the Report Writer's Peer Comparison section populated.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sentiment Analyst">
    **Channels**, all four on by default: news tone, Reddit trading communities, insider activity (Form 4 filings, which a company insider must file within two business days of trading their own stock), and options flow. You cannot turn all four off.

    **Depth**: Quick (headlines only), Standard (default), or Deep (full social scan, insider timeline, options analysis).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Chart Analyst">
    **Trading Mode**, which sets what the read looks for: Swing (days to weeks, default), Day Trade, or Scalp. **Timeframe**: Daily (default), 1H, 4H, Weekly. **Lookback Bars**: how much history is drawn, 20 to 200, default 60.

    **Indicators** drawn on the chart: RSI(14), MACD, Bollinger, EMA(20), Volume. All but Bollinger are on by default.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Market Brief">
    **Sections**, eleven available, five on by default: Portfolio Status, Watchlist Scan, Market Context, Catalysts, Top Idea. The rest are Crypto Analysis, Sector Rotation, Forex Monitor, Latest News, Macro Calendar, Earnings Radar. One must stay on.

    **Depth**: Concise, Standard (default), Detailed. **Watchlist** (optional): extra tickers to scan.

    This is the one agent needing no ticker. Set the asset type to Market and add a Schedule node.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Report Writer">
    **Sections**, grouped by what supplies them. Six always work: Executive Summary, Technical Analysis, Trade Plan, Position Sizing, Action Items, Scenario Analysis. The first four are pre-selected.

    The rest stay greyed out until the agent feeding them is on the canvas. Fundamental Analyst unlocks Fundamental Analysis, Earnings Outlook, Cash Flow Analysis, Peer Comparison. Research Agent unlocks Catalyst Calendar. Sentiment Analyst unlocks Options Strategy. Any one of the three unlocks Key Findings and Risk Assessment.

    Selections survive removing the agent that unlocked them, and the group header counts them.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Warning>
  A "Trade Plan" section is generated analysis, not instruction. Tradion cannot place trades and does not know your account.
</Warning>

## What comes back

Whatever the type: a direction (bullish, bearish, neutral), a conviction score from 1 to 10, a summary under 25 words, bullet-point sections, and exactly three key risks.

## What the agents can look at

There are 24 research tools and **every one is read-only**. None can place, change, or cancel an order. Each agent gets the subset fitting its job.

| Agent | Tools | Which ones |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research | 7 | Filing search and retrieval, transcripts, news, corporate actions, web search, page fetch |
| Fundamental | 7 | Statements, estimates, analytics, economic data, price, history, ETF profiles |
| Sentiment | 6 | News tone, Reddit, web search, page fetch, insider filings, options chain (with open interest, the contracts held open by somebody) |
| Market Brief | 20 | All but the filings and transcript tools, so crypto, forex, movers, market news |
| Report Writer | 2 | Price and history, to check a number handed to it |
| Chart Analyst | none | Reads a rendered chart image |

## What it costs

**One agent run per agent, per fire, except the Report Writer.** Three analysts is three runs per trigger. The Report Writer assembles what the others produced and costs nothing. Runs are refunded when an agent fails or times out. An account can also trigger only 20 agent-carrying automations an hour and 100 a day.

<Tip>
  The reliable way to burn an allowance is putting an agent on a high-frequency trigger. Watch the signal alone for a week in the [Runs tab](/automations/runs-and-history) first.
</Tip>

If the allowance runs out mid-month the automation keeps firing and messaging you, but the alert arrives without analysis and the run reads **degraded** or **failed**. Allowances reset on your billing date.

Trader accounts run Claude Sonnet, Quant accounts Claude Opus. The Chart Analyst is always Sonnet, because that is the model reading the image.

## Timing your agents

Two limits control how often agents run, on top of your monthly allowance.

**Burst cap:** 20 agent runs per hour and 100 per day, per account, across all automations combined. Hitting the hourly cap doesn't stop the alert — conditions are still checked every 60 seconds and the Action node still fires — but the agents are skipped and the run records as **degraded** or **triggered\_only** in history. It resets on the rolling window, not at midnight.

**Cooldown:** The Action node offers five presets — 5m, 15m, 1h (default), 4h, 1d. A cooldown mutes re-alerting, not re-checking: conditions keep being evaluated on the 60-second cycle, and the next match after the cooldown expires fires the full agent sequence again. Think of it as "don't spend an agent run on this more than once per N."

### Quick sizing guide

| Signal timeframe | Recommended cooldown | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1-day bars | 4h–1d | A daily RSI or price condition changes once a day; a shorter cooldown rarely does extra work |
| 1H bars | 1h–4h | A few alerts before the story has changed is enough |
| Sub-hour bars | 1d | The Market Brief alone takes ~6 min; a sub-hourly scalp setup has moved on before it lands |

Before attaching any agent, run the automation with signals only for a week and count the matches in the [Runs tab](/automations/runs-and-history). More than four or five matches a day on Trader — or forty on Quant — usually means the threshold needs tightening before agents go on.
