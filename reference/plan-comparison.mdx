Starter is every analysis tool with no monthly cap, plus the AI Portfolio Analyst. Trader adds AI Earnings Research. Quant is Trader with much bigger allowances: double the AI Portfolio Analyst messages and three times the AI Earnings Research messages.

## Everything that differs

|  | Starter | Trader | Quant |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| Price | $25/mo | $100/mo | $200/mo |
| Free trial | 7 days | 7 days | None |
| Chart Analysis and Lens | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Trade Autopsy and Playbook | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Portfolio and brokerage sync | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Tradion Memory | Included | Included | Included |
| AI Portfolio Analyst | 150 messages / mo | 500 / mo | 1,000 / mo |
| AI Earnings Research | Not included | 50 messages / mo | 150 / mo |

Reading that table:

- **AI Portfolio Analyst messages** are messages you send in the AI Portfolio Analyst portfolio chat. An **earnings message** is one question you send in AI Earnings Research.

Nothing outside this table is metered. Every row that reads *Unlimited* stays unlimited on all three plans, and the counters are the two rows with a monthly number: AI Portfolio Analyst messages and AI Earnings Research messages.

## Free trial

Starter and Trader include a **7-day free trial**. You enter card details up front and are not charged until day 8; cancel any time before then at no cost.

<Warning>
  **Quant has no trial.** The compute cost of its allowances makes a free week unviable. If you want to evaluate Tradion before committing at that level, trial Trader first and upgrade, the upgrade grants Quant's full allowance immediately.
</Warning>

The trial is once per account, not once per plan.

## Which one

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Choose Starter if…" icon="seedling">
    You want the analysis and behavioural tools and you don't need company earnings research. Every chart and autopsy tool is unlimited here, what you give up is AI Earnings Research.

    This is the right starting point for most people.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Choose Trader if…" icon="bolt">

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Choose Quant if…" icon="crown">
    You use AI Earnings Research daily and 50 messages a month is the binding constraint, or you chat with the AI Portfolio Analyst more than 500 times a month. The jump is bigger allowances on both meters, not a feature unlock. If you aren't hitting Trader's ceilings, you don't need it yet.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Changing plans

From **Settings → Subscription**:

- **Upgrades** apply immediately, and the new tier's full monthly allowance is granted right away rather than pro-rated.
- **Downgrades** reduce your allowances to the new tier's caps. The confirmation screen tells you when the change takes effect and what, if anything, you are charged. Read it before confirming.
- **Cancellation** keeps access until the period ends, then the account moves to unsubscribed. Your data is retained.

## There is no free plan

Three plans, all paid. Starter and Trader come with a 7-day free trial; there is no permanently free tier, and an account with no subscription cannot run analyses. If you see a "Free" plan mentioned anywhere, it is out of date.
