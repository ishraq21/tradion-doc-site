Starter and Pro include the exact same features. Pro's allowances are roughly double Starter's across the board, and Pro adds priority support.

## Everything that differs

|  | Starter | Pro |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| Price | $50/mo | $100/mo |
| Free trial | 3 days | 3 days |
| Tradion Snipe | 40 / mo | 60 / mo |
| Tradion Lens | 20 / mo | 30 / mo |
| Trade Autopsy | 20 / mo | 30 / mo |
| Portfolio and brokerage sync | Not metered | Not metered |
| Tradion Memory | Included | Included |
| AI Portfolio Analyst | 120 messages / mo | 250 / mo |
| Earnings Deep Research | 10 messages / mo | 25 / mo |
| Manual Profile refreshes | 15 / mo | 30 / mo |
| Priority support | Not included | Included |

Reading that table:

- **AI Portfolio Analyst messages** are messages you send in the AI Portfolio Analyst portfolio chat. An **earnings message** is one question you send in Earnings Deep Research. A **Tradion Snipe** or **Tradion Lens** count is one submission, however many images it includes. A **Trade Autopsy** count is one generated report. A **manual Profile refresh** is one use of the Refresh button on your Profile.

Portfolio and brokerage sync run on the house on both plans, along with follow-up questions on an analysis you already ran.

## Free trial

Both plans include a **3-day free trial** for new subscribers. You enter card details up front, and the trial gives you a smaller allowance of each counter than the full monthly amount above: enough to try every feature, not enough to run a full month on. You are not charged until the trial ends; cancel any time before then at no cost.

<Warning>
  If your first payment fails at the end of the trial, your account stays on its remaining trial allowance while Stripe retries the charge, rather than losing access outright.
</Warning>

The trial is once per account, not once per plan.

## Which one

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Choose Starter if…" icon="seedling">
    You check in on the market a few times a day. 40 Tradion Snipe analyses, 20 Tradion Lens analyses and 20 Trade Autopsies a month cover most working routines, and 10 Earnings Deep Research messages covers a handful of names.

    This is the right starting point for most people.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Choose Pro if…" icon="crown">
    You regularly run past one of Starter's six counters before the month resets: for example more than 120 AI Portfolio Analyst messages, or more than 10 Earnings Deep Research messages. The jump is roughly double every allowance plus priority support, not a feature unlock. If you aren't hitting Starter's ceilings, you don't need it yet.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Changing plans

From **Settings → Subscription**:

- **Upgrading** to Pro charges the prorated difference immediately, and once that charge succeeds you get Pro's full monthly allowance right away.
- **Downgrading** to Starter takes effect immediately. You keep what you have left, capped at Starter's limits, and the prorated difference is credited toward future invoices rather than refunded in cash.
- Switching plans during a trial ends the trial and charges you immediately for the new plan.
- **Cancellation** keeps access until the period ends, then the account moves to unsubscribed. Your data is retained.

## There is no free plan

Two plans, both paid. Starter and Pro both come with a 3-day free trial; there is no permanently free tier, and an account with no subscription cannot run analyses. If you see a "Free" plan mentioned anywhere, it is out of date.
