The notebook is where AI Earnings Research keeps what you believe next to the evidence, so you can test your view against reported results instead of remembering it kindly.

<Info>
  Part of [AI Earnings Research](/research/ai-earnings-research), included on **Trader** and **Quant**. Saving to the notebook does not use a message. Only questions do.
</Info>

Open it with **Notebook** in the top bar. It has five tabs.

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Theses** | A view you wrote, what would prove it wrong, and the evidence behind it |
| **Forecasts** | Revenue and profit scenarios you saved from the **Scenarios** view |
| **Transcripts** | Earnings-call passages saved during research |
| **Estimates** | Snapshots of analyst estimates, with when each was captured |
| **Prices** | Market prices saved during research, kept apart from earnings conclusions |

Transcripts, Estimates and Prices fill in by themselves as your questions fetch them. You write Theses and Forecasts.

## Write a thesis

A **thesis** is a claim about a company you can later confirm or drop, such as "this company's margins will keep widening".

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start from an answer, or from scratch">
    Under any answer, **Save as Thesis** opens the form with the answer's evidence filled in. Or choose **New Thesis** in the notebook.
  </Step>
  <Step title="State the view">
    **Your Thesis** is the only required field. **Assumptions**, **Supporting Evidence** and **What Would Invalidate It?** are optional, and the last one is worth filling in. A view with no condition that would end it cannot be tested.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a condition, if you can measure one">
    Choose a measure (operating margin, cash conversion, stock compensation as a share of revenue, revenue or free cash flow), **Above** or **Below**, and a number. This is the line that, if crossed, invalidates your thesis.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="The thesis form. Only the thesis itself is required. The condition at the bottom lets Tradion score it for you.">

</Frame>

## Check it when results arrive

Open the record and choose **Check Against Selected Research**. Tradion compares your condition with the latest reported quarter in the research you have open and saves the result with the date.

| Result | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Invalidation Condition Met** | The reported figure crossed your line |
| **Condition Not Met** | It did not |
| **Unknown** | The company did not report that measure |

<Warning>
  Only a condition with a number is checked automatically. Your written assumptions are not. They need you to read the evidence and decide.
</Warning>

## Forecasts

In **Scenarios**, enter a revenue growth, a net margin and the quarter you are aiming at, then **Save Forecast** with your assumptions. It is your scenario, not a prediction. Tradion assigns it no probability.

When that quarter is reported, a check compares your predicted revenue and net income with the actual figures. Two outcomes stop it from being scored:

- **Awaiting Results.** No matching quarter has been reported yet. A quarter counts as a match when it ends within 10 days of your target.
- **Retrospective; Excluded.** You saved the forecast on or after the quarter ended, so it is left out. A forecast only means something if you saved it before the results were known.

Actual figures use the latest filed values, including any restatement, so a score can shift slightly if a company revises a past quarter.

### In plain English

A forecast made after the answer is known is not a forecast. The notebook refuses to score those, so your record holds only views you took before the answer was known.

## Editing and deleting

Edit a thesis from its record. If you changed it in another tab, Tradion asks you to reload before saving, so one tab cannot overwrite the other. **Delete** asks for a second click to confirm.
