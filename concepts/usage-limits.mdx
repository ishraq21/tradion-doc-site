## Monthly allowances

| Counter | Starter | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI Portfolio Analyst messages | 120 | 250 |
| Earnings Deep Research messages | 10 | 25 |
| Tradion Snipe analyses | 40 | 60 |
| Trade Autopsies | 20 | 30 |
| Tradion Lens analyses | 20 | 30 |
| Manual Profile refreshes | 15 | 30 |

<Note>
  During a free trial, a smaller trial allowance applies instead: 15 / 1 / 3 / 2 / 3 / 1 on Starter and 30 / 3 / 5 / 3 / 5 / 2 on Pro, in the same row order as the table above. The full monthly allowance starts once your first payment succeeds, at the end of the trial.
</Note>

## What is *not* metered

These run as often as you like on any paid plan or trial:

- Portfolio dashboard refreshes and brokerage syncs
- Follow-up questions on a Tradion Snipe, Tradion Lens, or Trade Autopsy you already ran
- Autopsy intake (the narrative you type before a report is generated)
- The automatic Profile recomputation that runs after a Memory reset (only using the Refresh button yourself counts)

## When they reset

All six counters reset on your **billing cycle date**, the day of the month you subscribed, not the 1st. You can see your next reset date and current balances in **Settings → Monthly Usage**.

  Unused allowance **does not roll over**. A month where you used 4 of 10 earnings messages still resets to 10, not 16.

## What happens at zero

You get a clear block, not a silent failure. An upgrade prompt appears naming the specific counter you exhausted.

| Counter exhausted | What still works |
| --- | --- |
| AI Portfolio Analyst messages | Everything except sending a new message. Existing sessions stay readable. |
| Earnings Deep Research messages | Every earlier thread and your notebook stay readable; you can't send a new question. |
| Tradion Snipe, Tradion Lens analyses, or Trade Autopsies | Everything you already generated stays readable; you can't start a new one. |
| Manual Profile refreshes | Your existing Profile stays as-is; the automatic recomputation on your next tracked activity still works. |

Your options are to wait for the reset, or upgrade to Pro, which charges the prorated difference immediately and grants the full Pro allowance right away, rather than pro-rating the allowance itself.

## What each counter really counts

An **AI Portfolio Analyst message** is one message *you* send in the portfolio chat. The assistant's reply, and any internal tool calls it makes to read your holdings or pull quotes, are all free. The whole exchange counts as one message.

An **Earnings Deep Research message** is one question you send. Follow-ups count too, and so does a question that names three companies, so a wide question costs the same as a narrow one. A run that fails on Tradion's side gives the message back. **Stop** does not: the message is still counted, and **Ask Again** after a stop is a new question. The balance sits in the top bar of Earnings Deep Research as well as in **Settings → Monthly Usage**.

A **Tradion Snipe** or **Tradion Lens** count is one submission, regardless of how many images you include in it. A failed submission (a queueing error, or a job that errors or times out) gives the count back; leaving the page before it finishes does not.

A **Trade Autopsy** count is one generated report. Submitting the intake information, and asking follow-up questions about a completed report, are free. Any error during generation gives the count back.

A **manual Profile refresh** is one use of the Refresh button. A failed or empty result gives the count back.
