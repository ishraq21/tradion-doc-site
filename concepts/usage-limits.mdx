Tradion meters two things. Everything else on your plan is unlimited.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Chat messages" icon="message">
    Messages to the **AI Portfolio Analyst**, your portfolio chat.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Earnings messages" icon="magnifying-glass-chart">
    Questions you send in **AI Earnings Research**.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Monthly allowances

| Meter | Starter | Trader | Quant |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chat messages | 150 | 500 | 1,000 |
| Earnings messages | None | 50 | 150 |

<Note>
  Starter has no earnings messages because it doesn't include AI Earnings Research at all. It's the analysis-tools tier: Chart Analysis, Lens, and Trade Autopsy, all unlimited.
</Note>

## What is *not* metered

These run as often as you like on any paid plan:

- Chart Analysis analyses
- Lens captures
- Trade logging and autopsy generation
- Portfolio dashboard refreshes and brokerage syncs

## When they reset

Both counters reset on your **billing cycle date**, the day of the month you subscribed, not the 1st. You can see your next reset date and current balances in **Settings → Monthly Usage**.

<Frame caption="Settings → Monthly Usage shows live balances and the reset date.">
  <img src="/images/diagrams/usage-meters.svg" alt="Two meter rows, chat messages and earnings messages, each with a progress bar showing how much of the monthly allowance is used" noZoom />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  Unused credits **do not roll over**. A month where you used 4 of 50 earnings messages still resets to 50, not 96.
</Warning>

## What happens at zero

You get a clear block, not a silent failure. An upgrade prompt appears naming the specific meter you exhausted and the tier that would cover it.

| Meter exhausted | What still works |
| --- | --- |
| Chat messages | Everything except new AI Portfolio Analyst messages. Existing sessions stay readable. |
| Earnings messages | Every earlier thread and your notebook stay readable; you can't send a new question. |

Your options are to wait for the reset, or upgrade, which grants the new tier's full allowance immediately rather than pro-rating it.

## What each meter really counts

An AI Portfolio Analyst message is one message *you* send in the portfolio chat. The assistant's reply, and any internal tool calls it makes to read your holdings or pull quotes, are all free. The whole exchange counts as one message.

An earnings message is one question you send in AI Earnings Research. Follow-ups count too, and so does a question that names three companies, so a wide question costs the same as a narrow one. A run that fails on Tradion's side gives the message back. **Stop** does not: the message is still counted, and **Ask Again** after a stop is a new question. The balance sits in the top bar of AI Earnings Research as well as in **Settings → Monthly Usage**.
