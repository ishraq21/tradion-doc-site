Both plans include the same features: [AI Portfolio Analyst](/portfolio/ai-portfolio-analyst), [Earnings Deep Research](/research/ai-earnings-research), [Tradion Snipe](/research/chart-analyzer), [Tradion Lens](/research/lens), [Trade Autopsy](/trade-intelligence/trade-autopsy), and [Tradion Memory](/concepts/tradion-memory). Nothing is locked behind Pro. The difference is how much of each you get every month.

| Plan | Monthly allowance | Extra |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Starter** · $50/mo | 120 AI Portfolio Analyst messages, 10 Earnings Deep Research messages, 40 Tradion Snipe analyses, 20 Trade Autopsies, 20 Tradion Lens analyses, 15 manual Profile refreshes | None |
| **Pro** · $100/mo | 250 AI Portfolio Analyst messages, 25 Earnings Deep Research messages, 60 Tradion Snipe analyses, 30 Trade Autopsies, 30 Tradion Lens analyses, 30 manual Profile refreshes | Priority support |

Both plans include a **3-day free trial** for new subscribers, with a smaller trial allowance than shown above. The two sections below are the long version of that table; every exact limit is on the [plan comparison](/reference/plan-comparison) page.

## Starter: everything, at a working pace

Starter is the whole product (analysis tools, research, and the behavioral layer underneath), sized for someone who checks in a few times a day rather than living in the app. 40 Tradion Snipe analyses and 20 Tradion Lens analyses a month cover a chart-heavy routine; 20 Trade Autopsies covers reviewing most of what you close.

You also get the [Portfolio](/portfolio/overview) dashboard with brokerage connections, the full behavioral [Profile](/trade-intelligence/profile), the playbook, and [Earnings Deep Research](/research/ai-earnings-research) for reading a company's quarter in plain English (10 messages a month, enough for a handful of names).

If you regularly run past one of Starter's six counters before the month resets, that is the signal to move to Pro, not a missing feature.

## Pro: the same product, more room

Pro adds no new feature. It roughly doubles every allowance (up to 250 AI Portfolio Analyst messages and 60 Tradion Snipe analyses a month) and adds priority support.

Start on Starter and move to Pro when you hit a limit. The app tells you which counter ran out, so there is nothing to guess. See [Usage limits](/concepts/usage-limits) for how the counters work and when they reset.

## The 3-day trial

**Both plans include a 3-day free trial** for new subscribers, once per account. A payment method is required to start one. During the trial you get a smaller allowance of each counter than the full monthly amount above: enough to try every feature, not enough to run a full month on. The full monthly allowance starts once your first payment succeeds, at the end of the trial.

<Warning>
  If your first payment fails at the end of the trial, your account stays on its remaining trial allowance while Stripe retries the charge, rather than losing access outright.
</Warning>

During a trial, a countdown badge sits at the bottom of the sidebar showing days remaining. Cancelling before day 3 costs nothing.

## Changing your mind

You can move up or down at any time from Settings. Upgrading charges the prorated difference immediately and grants Pro's full allowance right away; downgrading takes effect immediately too, keeps what you have left (capped at Starter's limits), and credits the prorated difference toward future invoices rather than refunding it in cash. Switching plans during a trial ends the trial and charges you immediately. Full detail: [Managing your subscription](/account/managing-subscription).
