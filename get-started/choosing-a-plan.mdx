There are three plans, and picking between them comes down to a single question:

> **Do you want AI Earnings Research?**

If no, Starter. If yes, Trader. Quant is Trader with much bigger monthly allowances. You move to it when you run out of room, not when you need a feature.

| Plan | Pick it when | What is metered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Starter** · $25/mo | You want the analysis tools: charts, Lens, trade autopsies, and a portfolio chat. | 150 AI Portfolio Analyst messages a month. Everything else you will touch daily is unlimited. |
| **Trader** · $100/mo | You also want Tradion to read a company's quarter for you. | 500 AI Portfolio Analyst messages and 50 AI Earnings Research messages a month. |
| **Quant** · $200/mo | You ran out of room on Trader. Same features, much bigger allowances. | 1,000 AI Portfolio Analyst messages and 150 AI Earnings Research messages a month. |

<Info>
  New to these terms? [Usage limits](/concepts/usage-limits#what-each-meter-really-counts) explains what counts as an **earnings message** and an **AI Portfolio Analyst message**.
</Info>

Every plan includes a 7-day free trial except Quant. The three sections below are the long version of that table; every exact limit is on the [plan comparison](/reference/plan-comparison) page.

## Starter: the analysis tools

Starter gives you the whole analysis side of Tradion, with no monthly cap on the parts you will use most. Chart Analysis, [Lens](/research/lens), and [Trade Autopsy](/trade-intelligence/trade-autopsy) are all unlimited.

You also get the [Portfolio](/portfolio/overview) dashboard with brokerage connections, the full behavioural [Profile](/trade-intelligence/profile), the playbook, and [Tradion Memory](/concepts/tradion-memory) underneath all of it. The one metered part is the [AI Portfolio Analyst](/portfolio/ai-portfolio-analyst) (the chat window that can see your real holdings) at 150 messages a month.

What Starter does not have is **AI Earnings Research**. You bring the chart or the question about your own account; Tradion answers it. That suits you if you sit down with the market at set times, work through a handful of names, and review after the close.

## Trader: adds earnings research

Trader adds [AI Earnings Research](/research/ai-earnings-research). You ask about a company's quarter in plain English. Tradion reads its SEC filings, its earnings call and the analyst estimates, and answers with every figure tied to its source. Under the answer sit the company's financials, a read on management's tone, and a notebook for saving a thesis you can check when results arrive.

It is metered at 50 messages a month, where a message is one question you send. Follow-ups count too. AI Portfolio Analyst messages rise to 500 a month.

Trader suits you if you trade around earnings, or you want a written, sourced read on a company before you put money in it.

## Quant: the same product, more room

Quant adds no new feature. It raises the two allowances: 150 AI Earnings Research messages (three times Trader's) and 1,000 AI Portfolio Analyst messages (double), a month, and includes priority support.

Start on Trader and move to Quant when you hit a limit. The app tells you which meter ran out and which tier covers it, so there is nothing to guess. See [Usage limits](/concepts/usage-limits) for how the counters work and when they reset.

## The 7-day trial

**Starter and Trader both include a 7-day free trial. Quant does not.** Quant is billed immediately, because a free week on that compute allowance is not something the plan can absorb.

<Warning>
  The trial is once per account, not once per plan. Trialling Starter and then switching to Trader means the Trader subscription starts billing straight away. If you think you want AI Earnings Research, trial Trader.
</Warning>

During a trial, a countdown badge sits at the bottom of the sidebar showing days remaining. Cancelling before day 7 costs nothing.

## Changing your mind

You can move up or down at any time from Settings. Upgrading takes effect immediately; downgrading takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for. Full detail: [Managing your subscription](/account/managing-subscription).
