Tradion is one page with a fixed sidebar. The sidebar never changes and the main area swaps out under it.

**Home** and the **AI Portfolio Analyst** sit at the top, then two labelled groups: **My Trading** and **Research**. **Settings**, Documentation, Feedback, and Logout sit below a divider at the bottom, and a status bar follows you across most screens. Everything below is in that order. On a narrow tablet screen the sidebar becomes a bar along the bottom, with a **More** button that holds the rest.

## Home

The screen you land on every time you sign in. A launchpad, not a dashboard: nothing here is analysis to act on; everything is a way back into work you started.

Four bands, top to bottom:

**The header.** Your first name, and a pill showing whether the US market is open, in pre-market, or closed. Connect a brokerage and you also get a snapshot, total value and today's change. Without one, that tile is left out.

**Your numbers.** Two tiles pulled from elsewhere in the product:

| Tile | What it is | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Risk score | A 0–100 score across your entry quality, exit discipline, and risk management. Higher means safer trading | [Profile](/trade-intelligence/profile) |
| Win rate | The share of your logged trades that closed in profit | Your trade log |

They stay empty until you have logged trades, which is expected on day one and not a fault.

**Recent sessions.** Your AI Portfolio Analyst conversations, newest first. Clicking one takes you back into the Analyst, so last week's question is a starting point, not a retype.

**The launcher.** A card for four of the tools, each with a one-line description, and under them a **Research** row for the rest. You never need to remember which group a tool sits in.

## AI Portfolio Analyst

A chat window that can see your real positions. Because it reads live holdings rather than guessing, you can ask concrete things (how concentrated you are in one sector, what a 20% fall in one name would do to the portfolio) and the numbers reconcile with your broker. Sessions are saved; answers come with charts.

→ [AI Portfolio Analyst](/portfolio/ai-portfolio-analyst)

## My Trading

### Portfolio

Everything you hold: an equity curve (a line chart of the account's value over time) with 1W to All-time ranges, day and total profit and loss, sector allocation with a diversification score, a sortable holdings table with 10-session mini charts, and your biggest movers. It fills in once you connect a brokerage, a read-only link, so Tradion sees positions but places no orders.

→ [Portfolio overview](/portfolio/overview) · [Connecting a brokerage](/portfolio/connect-brokerage)

### Trade Autopsy

Where a closed trade gets taken apart. Log it (typed out in plain English, or imported from your broker) then run an autopsy. You get a root-cause breakdown, a 0–100 scorecard across entry quality, exit discipline, and risk management, and a counterfactual: the same trade replayed with one thing changed. Repeat findings become named patterns, and patterns become playbook rules that check you before the next trade.

→ [Trade Autopsy](/trade-intelligence/trade-autopsy) · [Logging a trade](/trade-intelligence/logging-a-trade)

### Profile

Your trading health check, assembled from everything else: a risk score, recurring mistakes ranked by severity and frequency, win rate by time of day, a year-long calendar of wins and losses, your goals, and your playbook rules. After two autopsies it unlocks a psychological profile, trader archetype, cognitive biases (predictable thinking errors, like holding losers longer than winners) with evidence from your own trades, and how you behave under stress.

→ [Profile](/trade-intelligence/profile)

## Research

### AI Earnings Research

A chat that answers questions about a company's quarter. Ask "Did AMD beat expectations last quarter?" and Tradion reads the company's SEC filings, its earnings call and the analyst estimates, then writes back with every figure tied to its source. Under the answer sit the company's financials, a management tone read, and a notebook for saving a thesis you can check when results arrive.

<Info>AI Earnings Research needs the **Trader** plan or above.</Info>

→ [AI Earnings Research](/research/ai-earnings-research)

### Chart Analysis

Drop in a saved chart image and get a BUY, SELL, HOLD, or NO TRADE verdict with a 0–100 confidence score, an entry price, a stop-loss (where you'd exit if it goes against you), a target, and the risk/reward ratio. The score breaks into the factors behind it, so you can see what the call rests on.

→ [Chart Analysis](/research/chart-analyzer) · [Reading a verdict](/concepts/reading-a-verdict)

### Lens

Lens does the same job without the file. It opens a full-screen live chart inside Tradion, set the symbol, timeframe, and indicators, then click **Capture**. Back comes a verdict, a summary, bull/base/bear price targets, and support and resistance levels (prices where buying or selling has tended to show up before). Follow-up questions are allowed.

Lens reads its own chart, not your other tabs. For an image you already have, use Chart Analysis.

→ [Lens](/research/lens)

## Settings

Pinned at the bottom of the sidebar: display name, subscription and billing, connected brokerages, account deletion. During a free trial a countdown badge appears above it. Under it sit **Feedback**, which attaches a note to the screen you were on, and **Logout**.

→ [Settings](/account/settings) · [Managing your subscription](/account/managing-subscription)

## The status bar

Three controls follow you across most screens. The AI Portfolio Analyst and AI Earnings Research run edge to edge, so the bar is hidden there:

- **The news wire**, a live headline ticker you can open for the full feed.
- **Brokerage**. Each connected broker, its accounts, when it last synced, and a sync button.
- **Personalization**, whether Tradion Memory feeds your history into AI answers. Off means generic analysis. It applies everywhere, immediately. The same switch is mirrored on your Profile.

→ [Tradion Memory](/concepts/tradion-memory)
